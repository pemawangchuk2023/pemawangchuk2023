## 🤠 About Me

I'm a finance professional by trade and a self-taught programmer by obsession — currently stitching those two worlds together, one repo at a time.

- 💼 **Finance-first thinking.** Years in accounting shaped how I approach systems: precision, discipline, and a healthy distrust of anything that isn't verifiable.
- 🧠 **Self-taught builder.** No CS degree, just curiosity and consistent reps in TypeScript, Solidity, and Next.js.
- 📜 **Bitcoin historian.** I'm fascinated by Bitcoin's origin story — from the mystery of **Satoshi Nakamoto** to the foundational work of pioneers like **Nick Szabo** — and how that history informs decentralized systems today.

> 🎯 **Mission:** keep learning accounting, smart contracts, and web development — with a focus on TypeScript, JavaScript, Python, and Solidity — while building real things with Next.js and TanStack.
